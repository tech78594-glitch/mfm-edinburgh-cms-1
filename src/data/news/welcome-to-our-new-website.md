We have refreshed the MFM Edinburgh website so you can find what you need quickly, on any device.

- **Service times** are on the home page, with the next service shown at the top.
- **Events** list everything coming up, from Power Must Change Hands to our All-Night Vigil.
- **Videos** let you catch up on services you missed.

If something is missing or wrong, email [info@mfmedinburgh.org](mailto:info@mfmedinburgh.org).
