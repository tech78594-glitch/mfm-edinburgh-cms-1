What to expect on a Sunday

Come as you are. Someone will greet you at the door and help you find a seat.

  1. Arrive by 09:30 for the Pentecostal Hour, or by 10:30 for the main service.
  2. Expect vibrant praise, Bible teaching and fervent prayer. The main service runs for about three hours.
