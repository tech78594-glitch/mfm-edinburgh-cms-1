Who we are

A full gospel church that believes God still delivers

MFM Edinburgh is part of Mountain of Fire and Miracles Ministries, a worldwide full gospel ministry founded in Lagos in 1989. We preach salvation, practise holiness within and without, and minister deliverance.

Here you will learn to pray for yourself. We call it a do-it-yourself gospel: every believer is trained to stand, pray and see God move.
