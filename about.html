Preaching salvation, practising holiness, ministering deliverance

MFM Edinburgh is a non-denominational, Spirit-filled fellowship and part of Mountain of Fire and Miracles Ministries worldwide.

Who we are

Raising an army of prayer warriors

We are committed to raising believers who pray with power and expose the works of darkness, ready for the end-time revival.

MFM is a full gospel ministry devoted to the revival of apostolic signs, Holy Ghost fire and the unlimited power of God to deliver to the uttermost. Absolute holiness, within and without, is taught openly as the greatest safeguard and a condition for heaven.

We see aggressive prayer as an aid to spiritual focus. At MFM Prayer City, prayer continues 24 hours a day, 7 days a week. We also believe that sound Bible knowledge is essential for good Christian living, and that Spirit-filled praise brings the presence of God among us.

Visit us and experience God like never before.

Our name

Mountain. Fire. Miracles.

  • Mountain

    The mountain top is a place of victory, above the powers of darkness, where God’s servants do exploits.

  • Fire

    “He shall baptize you with the Holy Ghost, and with fire” (Matthew 3:11). The baptism of fire is the best thing that can happen to anyone.

  • Miracles

    When you reach the mountain top and receive the fire, miracles follow.

Our story

From a living room to every continent

  1. 1989Dr Daniel Kolawole Olukoya calls a Wednesday prayer meeting in his living room. 24 people attend, and verifiable miracles follow.
  2. GrowthThe meetings move to 60 Old Yaba Road, then to 13 Olasimbo Street, Onike, Yaba — a former slum that becomes the International Headquarters.
  3. 1994The name Mountain of Fire and Miracles Ministries is revealed in prayer, and the church holds its first service on 24 April.
  4. TodayBranches across Nigeria, then London, the US, Canada, Europe, the Caribbean, Africa and Asia — including our congregation here in Edinburgh.

Our mission

To spread the gospel of our Lord Jesus Christ all over the world.

Our vision

  • Revive apostolic signs, wonders and miracles
  • Gather children of God who have been lost in dead churches
  • Train believers in the art and science of spiritual warfare
  • Help believers receive the Holy Ghost baptism and fire, and walk daily with the Holy Spirit
  • Build an aggressive, victorious end-time army for the Lord
  • Deliver those who have become slaves to pastors, prophets and apostles
  • Build heaven-bound Christians — our priority is that people make heaven
  • Build up prayer eagles, and purify the Pentecostal dirtiness of this age
What we believe

Our statement of faith

  1. The Scriptures are the inspired Word of God, the only basis for our faith and fellowship
  2. The fall of mankind, requiring redemption through the Blood of Jesus Christ
  3. Salvation through the redeeming work of Jesus Christ and the regenerating work of the Holy Spirit; sanctification as separation from evil
  4. The Baptism of the Holy Spirit (Acts 2:4; 10:44; 19:1–6)
  5. Restitution for past wrongs where possible
  6. The ordinances of the Lord’s Supper and Water Baptism
  7. The Church Universal, visible and invisible
  8. A ministry divinely called and scripturally ordained
  9. Divine healing, as provided by the Lord Jesus Christ
  10. The Rapture, the Marriage Supper of the Lamb and the second coming of Christ
  11. The Millennial Reign of Christ and the Final Judgment
  12. The New Heaven and the New Earth

Hold your peace and let the Lord fight your battle as you worship with us.
